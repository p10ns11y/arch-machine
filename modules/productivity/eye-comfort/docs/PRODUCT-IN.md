## Product Purpose

**India overlay** structures eye-comfort SoT with:

- **Ṛtu (six seasons)** — Śiśira → Vasanta → Grīṣma → Varṣā → Śarad → Hemanta (year context)
- **Day** — maps to 7 circadian phases; optional prahar labels (like TN jāmam)
- **Region identity packages** — Gangetic, Himalaya, Deccan, coastal, desert hue leans

TN remains the landscape-poetry calendar; India is the broader civil/seasonal mode. Operators switch calendars; packages never overwrite `eye-comfort-tn-*`.

Success = AA contrast first; India as climate and land, not festival neon.

## Brand Personality

Calm · Warm · Precise — monsoon dust, mountain mist, plateau heat, coastal air (restrained).

## Anti-references

Parent + TN-adjacent anti-references, plus:

- Holi / festival RGB as default syntax
- Temple-gold chrome UI
- Pure white “AC summer” glare
- Collapsing all India into one tinai or one stereotype

## Design Principles

1. **Eye comfort first.**
2. **TN coexistence** — separate calendar id and package prefix.
3. **Region packages stable**; ṛtu live-renders.
4. **Warm slate teal** for water/coast, not cool cyber blue.
5. **Wallpaper is stage** — monsoon field, ridge mist, plateau calm.
