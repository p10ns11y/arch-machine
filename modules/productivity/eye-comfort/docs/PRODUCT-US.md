## Users

Same Omarchy operator; optional US cultural calendar — early-republic desk mood, plain craft, or modern American range/civic scenes. Long sessions; chrome quiet.

## Product Purpose

**America overlay** structures eye-comfort SoT with:

- **1776 / early republic spine** — parchment desk, iron-gall ink, hearth lamp, field and workshop
- **Amish and other plain craft** — undyed linen, iron oxide, low chroma “Ordnung-calm” intensity option
- **Modern America welcome** — cowboy/range, contemporary desk, **flag/eagle civic motifs when AA-clean and restrained** (not banned)

Indigenous nation calendars are **out of v1** (optional later EC-US-INDIG with sources).

Success = AA contrast first; American story as light and material, not unreadable patriotism spam.

## Brand Personality

Calm · Warm · Precise — parchment craft + honest dust-road dusk when range is on.

## Anti-references

Parent anti-references, plus:

- Pure #000/#FFF frontier extremes
- Unreadable low-contrast flag RGB or seal-on-seal noise (symbols **ok** when high-contrast and quiet)
- Sacred Indigenous symbol decoration in v1

**Explicitly allowed when good:** cowboy/western range themes; modern US desk moods; restrained civic color.

## Design Principles

1. **Eye comfort first.**
2. **1776 + plain = default spine**; range/modern are first-class package options.
3. **Identity packages** (~6) — seasons live-render.
4. **Civic symbols earn their place** via contrast and restraint, not volume.
5. **Wallpaper is stage** — field, hearth, workshop, open range calm fields.
